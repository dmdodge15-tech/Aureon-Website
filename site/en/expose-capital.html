The situation

When the house bank reaches its limits.

Exhausted credit lines, tied-up collateral, an equity gap or simply too little time: we know the typical bottlenecks and the funders who solve them.

Good ventures rarely fail on the idea. They fail on a capital structure that does not fit the project, or on the wrong counterpart.

Common equity

Most junior

Minority and majority stakes. Carries the highest risk and in return gets a say and the largest share of the upside.

Preferred Equity

Ahead of equity

Equity with a preferred return. Closes gaps without the shareholders giving up control.

Mezzanine

Subordinated

Subordinated capital that strengthens the equity base. Improves bankability and raises the financing ceiling considerably.

Senior Debt

First rank · secured

Conventional secured financing on the most favourable terms. Term and repayment matched to cash flow.

Documents

What we need for a first assessment.

A short description is enough for the first call. Before approaching funders, we compile the following documents with you. Whatever is missing, we work out together.

Projects & real estate Project

  • Project description & exposéLocation, use, concept
  • Capital need & use of funds
  • Cost and investment planincl. timeline
  • Land register extract & site plan
  • Building permit or planning status
  • Valuation, lease or operator concept
  • Proof of equity

Companies & M&A Company

  • Annual accounts for the last three years
  • Current management accounts and trial balance
  • Three to five year planP&L, liquidity, capex
  • Shareholder and group structure
  • Bank overview & existing financing
