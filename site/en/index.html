Fit for Future

Supply and warranty continue long after end of production.

Many suppliers stop planning at EOP. OEMs do not. Spare parts supply is typically required for around 15 years after end of production, and warranty terms are negotiated long before the first part ships. We make this part of your offer, not a surprise.

Selected mandates

Three problems, and what was left behind.

Mandate 01 Automotive safety
USD 1.2bn spend

Building a global commodity organisation

Situation
Purchasing ran plant by plant. No separation between plant, project and commodity responsibility, and no single owner for any supplier relationship.
Approach
Designed and rolled out a global commodity structure, classified the supplier base, installed a global sourcing committee, and led thirty commodity buyers through the change.
Result
One consolidated supplier portfolio, binding award governance, and price and contract negotiations steered centrally instead of repeated at every site.
Mandate 02 Group-wide
Metal & plastic parts

Supplier risk and award governance

Situation
No cross-site view of supplier risk. RFQ and award processes differed by plant, and the contract documents differed with them.
Approach
Selected the risk system and designed the process behind it, rolled out a global RFQ platform, and harmonised nomination contracts and NDAs into one standard.
Result
An award trail that survives an audit, and critical suppliers identified while there is still time to act on them.
Mandate 03 Electric parking brake
North American ramp-up

Localising core components on another continent

Situation
Core components shipped from Europe while a US plant ramped up: long lead times, currency exposure, and slow response whenever quality moved.
Approach
Supplier identification and development on the ground — qualification, negotiation and nomination of local sources for the motor-gearbox unit, drive wheel and worm.
Result
A local supply base, materially lower logistics and FX exposure, and issues resolved in hours rather than across a shipping week.
