4. Automatic language selection

When you open the start page, your country of origin is determined from your IP address in order to direct you to the German or English version. This happens at the moment of the request at our hosting provider. We do not store the result and we do not create profiles. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can choose the language yourself at any time with the switch at the top right.

Further information: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp we process the data you provide (for example name, contact details, content of your message) to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data once it is no longer needed and no retention duties apply.

The WhatsApp buttons on the site only open WhatsApp, operated by WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland, after you click. No data is transferred to WhatsApp before that. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to its use.

Only then is your IP address transmitted to Calendly, and Calendly may set cookies or similar technologies. If you book an appointment, Calendly processes the data you enter (for example name, email address, chosen time, optional message) and passes it to us so that we can arrange the call. The legal basis for loading the tool is your click, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and for the booking itself Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can always contact us by email instead.
